## The Midnight Note generation adaptation
This independent generation task uses Omni2Web119 and WebVR test/290/290_1.html. It uses different source HTML, review video and images from sample118/WebVR155 and the exploratory AETHER035/WebVR55. A complete browser-capture tour with temporary Chinese Huihui TTS is added. Nine external original images are localized, and one deterministic generated texture is supplied as an optional resource. Source HTML and original annotation are withheld from solvers. The original English human review is unaltered. The organizer reference fixes original mobile overflow; random particles and complex animation may be approximated. Separate calibrated visible-outcome grading is not official EFS. Retain applicable source and image notices separately.

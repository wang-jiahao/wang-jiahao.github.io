Questions, answered quietly

A few details—so your night moves without friction.

Is it a listening room?

Yes. Conversation is welcome between sets; during performances we keep service quiet and ask guests to keep voices low. Think supper-club energy with concert-level attention.

How long is a typical visit?

Plan for two sets and intermission—about 2 hours. Chef's tasting runs with the first set and finishes during the second. Lounge bookings are typically 2.5 hours.

Do you offer non-alcoholic pairings?

We do. The Breath & Citrus pairing focuses on botanicals, tea infusions, and carbonation—built to complement the menu without overwhelming the room.

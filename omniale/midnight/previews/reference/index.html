Listening Room Protocol

Our stage is mixed for clarity, not volume. Sets are framed by short intermissions—enough time to refresh a drink, not enough to break the spell.

  • House system tuned for upright bass definition and cymbal air.
  • Vinyl prelude from the club library before each first set.
  • Quiet service choreography; menus land between tunes.

Chef’s Set Menu: “Midnight Service”

Five courses built around late-night brightness—citrus, smoke, and slow heat—paired to the pacing of the evening’s program.

  • First course arrives with the opening theme—clean, bracing, precise.
  • Main course lands before the final solo—comforting and deep.
  • Dessert is plated during applause—quiet sweetness, no rush.

Room Atmosphere

Low tables, deep banquettes, and a ceiling designed to scatter reflections like brushwork—soft edges, clean detail. The room is calibrated for closeness: you’ll feel the cymbal swell before you notice it.

  • Candlelight speculars across glass and brass.
  • Sapphire-blue gradients in the lounge, velvet violet at the bar.
  • Seat spacing optimized for conversation between sets.

# RoomSound — evidence and limitations

## Provenance
This is an independent, provisional annotation of the provided synchronized 60-second STARSS23 room video and FOA recording (SONY / Tampere University). No official labels, actor IDs, reference answers, stems, external browsing or hidden annotations were used. Native listening by the agent is unavailable; audio interpretation uses the shared real-W-signal ASR/YAMNet and raw FOA intensity evidence. Consequently this is not a hand-audited transcription.

## Performer continuity and coordinates
`p1` is the nearer table performer, around source x=80 (azimuth +165°), mouth roughly elevation −12°. `p2` is the far-table performer, around x=270 (azimuth +130°), elevation −8°. Both remain seated at the same table over the clip. Their speech, laughter and clapping keep the same IDs. The video masks faces, so mouth motion and individual vocal identity are not reliably observable. The FOA high-band speech energy frequently points +125…+145°, supporting the table sector but not resolving the two people independently.

`p3` is the performer wearing the dark red top near the large door, x≈1040 at the opening (−15…−20°), then x≈960 (0°) from around 6s, approaching the central prop basket at 24–33s (+10…+30°), back near +6° by 39s. This performer interacts with small sounding objects around the basket; attribution of bell bursts to this track is plausible but not confirmed for every burst. A hand-held device near the opening complicates the 7–9s alarm-like sound. It is kept as a tentative bell episode rather than inventing a telephone subtype.

`background` is the one non-tracked agent for all room equipment, even when directions differ. Music has a coarse inferred back/overhead direction (−160°, +35°) from low-band energy during relatively quiet 5–10s passages. The room-equipment annotation at −100°, −20° is LOW confidence: the fan is visibly rotating, and quieter mechanism/room sound predictions recur, but neither proves independently audible whole-clip activity. q9 explicitly remains unresolved. Background markers are approximate sector indicators, not precise equipment localization.

Projection uses source x=(180−azimuth)/360×1920. Estimates are rounded to broad 5–15° scales, not claimed sub-degree measurements. Decimals in event timing express convenient seek values, not sample-accurate acoustic boundaries.

## Activity inferences
- **Music:** strong consistent evidence across all three windows, especially 5–11,18–24,29–35,41–43s. Very low music scores under foreground speech/applause do not imply the music ceased. One whole-clip mixed-background episode, no duplicate instrument label for the same recorded music.
- **Bell:** predictions at 1.44–2.4 (bell .23 / gong .12), 11.52–12.96 (ping/ding/bell), 16.32–17.76 (bell .29),20.64–21.6 (bell .90),25–28 (bell/tuning fork/chime),39.36–40.32,42.24–43.2. Boundaries extend approximately through decay. The 7.2–8.64 alarm/siren prediction is ambiguous and bell-02 is particularly uncertain. Moving trajectory for the basket passage follows p3, not raw mixed intensity peaks.
- **Speech:** strongest classifier evidence at 3.84–4.8,14.4–15.84,16.8–18.24,35.52–39.36,43.68–45.12,50.4–55.68,57.12–60. Short low-score possibilities at 23–25,28–31 are kept as provisional short intervals. Opening ASR says 'Thank you' then several phrases, but its no-speech probability is .5567 and music is dominant. The long ASR sequence about a house across 19–29s is inconsistent with strong music classification and should not be trusted as a literal transcript. Speech attribution to p1 vs p2 is weak, using the sector and table interaction timing, not demographic voice labels.
- **Laughter:** provisional vocal-burst interpretation at 10.8–12.9,33–34.5,44.7–46.8. Generic predictions are choir/chant/opera in the earlier bursts and dog/howl in the latter. Table interaction and posture changes support non-music vocal activity, but they are NOT enough to conclusively classify both people laughing at once. We submit these as uncertain hypotheses rather than quote the model's unrelated choir/dog categories. q1 and q6 are low-confidence candidates. Weak chuckle predictions at 51–52s occur under speech and were not duplicated as a new simultaneous source.
- **Clapping:** best grounded transient activity. Far-table hands together by 46.5s; both visibly clap at 47.5,49.5; clapping scores .68 at46.56, .99 at47.52, .96 at48.48, .97 at48.96, .75 at49.44. The final frame at50.5 has both hands low/near together. Start/end estimates 46.2–50.6 for p2 and47–50.5 for p1. Tap/door predictions in this region were not added as spurious doors.
- **Domestic:** quiet continuous equipment is a hypothesis, clearly marked low confidence above. No footsteps are annotated solely because p3 walks. No actual door opening/closing is visible at40–44s despite sliding-door predictions. No water, knock, separate instrument, or telephone activity is sufficiently supported to add those classes.

## Review derivation
Bookmarks use only existing inferred events. q2 is the clearest overlap; q10 is the clear speech-after-clapping transition. q4 nominates the opening speech as the longest submitted speech event but continuity/source attribution there is weak. q1/q6 use tentative earlier vocal laughter interpretation. q3/q5/q7/q8/q9 are explicitly unresolved because the overlapping performer activities or continuous second source cannot be established from the tools. Their candidate windows are documented for context only; the app does not navigate unresolved entries or claim them as answers. q5 counts five candidate emitters/activities, including two sounds attributed to the single background agent, not five reliably distinguished performer IDs.

## Implementation
Local HTML/CSS/JS only. Canvas crops exactly 120° horizontally and90° vertically, centered at elevation0; seam pieces wrap. The visible rectangle is4:3. Markers use the documented linear crop projection and shortest-arc interpolated azimuth. Panorama and marker placement work paused. No perspective or HRTF claim.

The explicit Play gesture resumes WebAudio and fetches/parses the original PCM16 four-channel WAV without browser downmixing. W,Y,Z,X are retained in a four-channel AudioBuffer; discrete splitter sends W,X,Y through six gain nodes to a two-channel merger. Gains implement L=.5(W−X sin yaw+Y cos yaw), R=.5(W+X sin yaw−Y cos yaw); Z is not connected. Master volume is after decoding. Silent video is the transport clock; audio buffer sources restart at seeks/resumes/loop boundaries, and drift over90ms is corrected. Neither mono nor fixed stereo assets are played. Annotations and filters never affect mixed audio.

Edits/imports validate before cloning/committing, with a single shared state transaction. Undo/redo, filters, markers, editor values, follow target, timeline and bookmark staleness derive from this state. JSON round-trip includes all fields. Browser localStorage persists current state against the original-document signature, avoiding accidentally loading old edits when the submitted events file changes. Reset is one atomic undoable change, stores the submitted state. Marker dragging edits the nearest keyframe with earlier-time tie break; non-selected markers are pointer-inert. Review freshness only tests referenced event/window overlap, not whether the hypothesis is correct.

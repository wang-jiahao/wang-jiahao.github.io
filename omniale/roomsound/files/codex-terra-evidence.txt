# Evidence and limitations

The supplied panorama frames at 1, 20 and 40 seconds show people near the left table and the central door. Their presence and posture are not evidence of sound. Performer-a is a tentative association, not a verified identity; multiple speakers or clappers may be merged. The background ID groups non-person sounds. Directions are rough estimates, not measured source tracks.

The shared YAMNet evidence repeatedly predicts music across the recording, with some interruptions by competing predictions. Speech scores rise around 3.8–4.8, 12–12.9, 14.4–15.8, 16.8–17.8, 35.5–38.9, 43.7–44.7 and 50.4–59.5 seconds. Clapping scores are strong from 46.6–49.9 seconds. Bell predictions occur near 1.4, 16.3, 20.6 and 25.4–26.9 seconds; these could be musical tones rather than independent bells. Intervals are approximate classifier windows, not hand-audited onset boundaries. The early ASR has high no-speech probability and may hallucinate under music; its words and speaker attribution are not used.

The FOA intensity evidence around 46–55 seconds is often near +130 to +160 degrees in mid and high bands. It combines sources and reflections, so the performer direction is only a coarse hypothesis. Music and bell directions are especially uncertain. The second continuous sound mentioned in the task cannot be identified from the supplied evidence with enough confidence to label it; review q9 and other unsupported overlaps are unresolved. q4 and q10 are provisional based on the estimated events, not verified ground truth. No native listening or official annotations were available.
